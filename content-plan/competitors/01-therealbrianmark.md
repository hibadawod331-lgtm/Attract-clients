# Competitor analysis: @therealbrianmark

**Data:** last 100 reels, pulled with Apify on 4 Nov 2026. The top 30 were transcribed and their covers checked.

**Account basics:**
- **Typical reel:** about 16K views (median).
- **Posting rate:** up to 12 reels a day (Oct–Nov 2026). He tells people to post 2 to 6 times a day.
- **Best length:** 15–30 seconds (median about 24K views). Under 15 seconds averages about 1.8K, so his very short reels flop, except one list reel.
- **Comment keyword CTA:** reels with "Comment GAME / SALES / ASTRA…" get about 22K views, against about 11.5K for reels without one. That's roughly double.

**Note:** the newest reels (3–4 Nov) are still gaining views, so they rank lower than they will end up.

## The patterns
1. **Show money numbers early.** "How much do you make?", "$10K client vs $1K client", "$100K as fast as possible". Money in the first second is his strongest trigger.
2. **Contrast between two people.** Broke vs rich, old vs new, cheap client vs premium client. Two people on screen, one is wrong.
3. **Lists and rankings.** 10/10, top X%, tier lists, "follow these people". They're easy to film, and people watch to the end to see number 1.
4. **Role-play sales situations.** Objections, DMs and calls, so the viewer learns a real script.
5. **Free resource plus comment keyword on most reels.** This doubles views, because the comments push reach.
6. **Raw emotion.** Rants and storytimes reacting to a comment.
